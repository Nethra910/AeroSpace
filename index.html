What the division is working toward

Three areas the team is building out first, in order of where work is currently focused.

Propulsion research

Grounding early propulsion work in first-principles fluid mechanics and compressor theory, developed alongside academic coursework in the field.

Amateur & experimental rocketry

An early-stage reusable rocketry program, built to operate within India's regulatory framework under the Explosives Act and Aircraft Act.

Biomimetic design principles

Treating natural systems as a design reference — the "Nature" half of the division's name — rather than an afterthought.

Team SIMHA

Building a reusable launch vehicle

Team SIMHA is the group developing MANI Aerospace's upcoming reusable rocket — the recovery and re-flight architecture underpinning the road to Vision 2036. Led by Project Director Nangireddy Manikanta, with 14 engineers across propulsion, structures and recovery systems.
