# MANI Aerospace visual brief

## Point of view

MANI Aerospace is presented as a calm mission-control room crossed with an
engineering drawing: dark, precise, editorial, and confident. Interfaces use
rules, coordinates, section indices, and measured whitespace as structure
rather than decoration.

## Tokens

- **Surfaces:** `#070b12` (base), `#0b111b` (raised), `#111a26` (panel),
  `#172333` (hover).
- **Ink:** `#f2f5f7` (primary), `#a7b3bf` (muted), `#6e7c89` (quiet).
- **Accents:** `#61d6d1` (signal teal), `#f0a35b` (warm status/CTA),
  `#7ed39c` (success), `#ef7777` (danger).
- **Type:** Space Grotesk for display and UI, IBM Plex Mono for labels,
  coordinates, metadata, and data.
- **Scale:** 12/14/16 body labels, 20/28 section headings, 48/72 display;
  headings use tight tracking and body copy uses generous line-height.
- **Spacing:** a 4px base, with 24px, 40px, 64px, and 96px section rhythm.
- **Radii:** 2px for controls, 6px for panels, 999px only for status pills.
- **Rules:** 1px hairlines at low contrast; accent appears on focus, active
  states, and primary actions only.

## Motion

Motion is short, directional, and functional: opacity, transforms, and
clip-path only. Page entry and reveals stay under 600ms, hover states are
150–250ms, and reduced-motion users receive a simple opacity transition.
Nothing delays access to content or interaction.
